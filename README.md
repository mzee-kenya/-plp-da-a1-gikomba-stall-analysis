## Project Overview

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and wants to understand how her business is performing, whether sales are growing, which products need restocking and where she should focus her efforts. She has maintained daily sales records for two weeks and keeps basic stock information, including product quantities, reorder levels, costs and selling prices. This project uses core Python to analyse her sales performance, compare weekly results, classify daily sales, examine weekday and weekend performance, identify products requiring restocking and calculate gross profit margins. The analysis provides practical, data-driven recommendations to help Wanjiku improve sales, manage inventory and make better business decisions.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis are to:

1. Calculate total, average, highest and lowest daily sales.
2. Compare sales performance between the two weeks and calculate growth.
3. Classify daily sales as Strong, Normal or Weak.
4. Compare average weekday and weekend sales.
5. Identify products that need restocking.
6. Calculate the gross margin percentage for each product.
7. Generate a formatted Stall Performance Report and provide business recommendations.

## Top Three Findings

Based on the analysis of Wanjiku's 14-day sales and stock records, the following findings were identified:

1. **Total Sales:** The stall generated total sales of **KES 46,450**, with an average daily sale of approximately **KES 3,317.86** over the 14-day period.

2. **Weekly Sales Growth:** Sales increased from **KES 22,050 in Week 1** to **KES 24,400 in Week 2**, representing a positive growth of **10.66%**.

3. **Weekend Performance:** Average weekend sales were **KES 4,900**, compared to average weekday sales of **KES 2,685**. This means weekend sales were approximately **82.50% higher** than weekday sales.

## Key Business Insights

- Four days were classified as Strong, four as Normal and six as Weak.
- Tomatoes and Sukuma were identified as products requiring restocking.
- Avocado recorded the highest gross margin at approximately 42.86%.
- Weekend sales performed considerably better than weekday sales.

## Recommendations

Based on the findings, Wanjiku should:

- Increase stock preparation before weekends to meet higher customer demand.
- Prioritise restocking Tomatoes and Sukuma because their quantities are at or below their reorder levels.
- Monitor weekly sales to understand whether the positive growth continues.
- Investigate weak sales days and consider promotions to attract more customers.
- Monitor product profitability and maintain a balanced inventory based on demand and gross margins.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that Wanjiku's business generated KES 46,450 in sales over two weeks, with a 10.66% increase in sales during the second week. Weekend sales performed significantly better than weekday sales, highlighting an opportunity to improve stock planning. By monitoring sales patterns, restocking products at the right time and focusing on profitability, Wanjiku can make more informed business decisions and improve her stall's performance.
